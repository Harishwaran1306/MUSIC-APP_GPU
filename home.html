A LITTLE GUIDANCE

Help & support

Helpful tips for getting the most from your music discovery experience.

How do I identify a song?

Choose Identify a Song, record a short melody, and review the preview. This prototype can record audio, but it does not include a real song-recognition engine.

How should I hum or whistle?

Choose a clear, distinctive part of the melody. Keep background noise low and try a 10–20 second phrase. Lyrics are not required for melody matching in a future integrated engine.

How do I upload a clip?

Open Upload Audio, choose or drop a supported audio file, then use the local playback preview. Demo analysis displays a clearly labelled no-match state.

Why can't TamilTune identify my song?

The current deliverable is a frontend prototype. No live music catalog, fingerprint service, melody model, or lyrics provider is connected.

Microphone troubleshooting

• Use a current version of Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.
• Open the site on localhost or HTTPS.
• Allow microphone access when prompted.
• Check that another app isn't exclusively using your microphone.
• Stop recording before closing the recording screen.

Need a simpler place to start?

Try the sample result to explore the interface without using your microphone.

OUR STORY

About TamilTune

Where Nature Meets Music, Forever.

Frontend prototype

Every tune holds a memory.

TamilTune imagines a warmer way to find music: through a melody you remember, a tune you hum, or a song you want to explore again.

Our mission

Connect people with the songs hidden in everyday moments, with a thoughtful, accessible experience inspired by nature and human emotion.

What this demo includes

Interactive navigation, sample catalogue cards, local favourites and history, language preference controls, audio recording and upload previews. Real AI recognition, lyrics retrieval, streaming, and account services are not connected.

Privacy & terms

Prototype notice

This local prototype saves preference, library, and activity data in your browser storage. Microphone access is initiated only when you choose to record. The sample result and sample catalogue are for UI demonstration only; they do not represent a verified recognition or ranking service.
